About

D. R. Dison is the founder of Artificial Intelligence University (AIU), an independent research organization, and the creator and owner of Causalontology, PrologAI, Mentova, and Konnectome.

Causalontology is the world's first synthesis of a reified, process-first causal calculus (its verbs), a continuant backbone of persisting entities (its nouns), and a realizable hinge that binds the two - one foundational, glass-box ontology, grounded in embodied, interventional learning.

PrologAI is one of the world's first complete glass-box cognitive substrates for building synthetic minds, with no neural network, no Large Language Model (LLM) and no black-box concerns; a new programming language written entirely in pure *SWI-Prolog - a base, a foundation, a substrate for anyone to build their own custom Glass-box Synthetic Mind (GSM), where every reasoning step is a named, inspectable rule and every answer comes with a justification tree you can read, uniting 48 named modes of reasoning in a single, fully inspectable architecture. (* Sociaal-Wetenschappelijke Informatica (Dutch for "Social Science Informatics"))

Mentova is the world's first Glass-box Synthetic Mind (GSM)**, written in PrologAI, to score 400/400 = 100% on the public training set of the Abstract Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence Version 1 (ARC-AGI-1) and 120/120 = 100% on the public data set of ARC-AGI-2. Every answer comes with a readable justification tree, no black box and no Large Language Model (LLM). (** Glass-box means every step can be read and audited, the opposite of a hidden black box. A Synthetic Mind means a machine built to reason, remember, and explain, rather than a statistical model that predicts text - a system that knows what it knows, knows how it knows it, and can show you the proof. Mentova is the world's first Glass-Box Synthetic Mind that only says what it can actually explain.)

Konnectome is the world's first Glass-box Synthetic Mind (GSM) to be based on the human nervous system. (Mentova was based on abstract cognitive architectures, not neuroscience.) Written in PrologAI and Causalontology 3.0.0 compliant, Konnectome is built from human brain neuroscience and the SPARCD*** Appendices of the book, "Nature's Cognitive Architecture: From Neuroscience to Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)." (*** Specification, Pseudo-code, Architecture, Refinement, Completion, Demonstration)
